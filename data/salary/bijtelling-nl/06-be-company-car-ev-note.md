# Electric company cars – note for Belgian clients

Author: Pieter Jacobs · Payroll BE · June 2026

In Belgium there is no bijtelling. The benefit in kind of a company car depends on CO2 emissions and catalogue value.

For a fully electric company car the CO2 percentage is 4%, applied to 6/7 of the catalogue value, with the yearly minimum benefit.
