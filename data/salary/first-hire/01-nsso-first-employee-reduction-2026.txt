TARGET GROUP REDUCTION – FIRST EMPLOYEE

Reference: NSSO/TGR/FIRST/2026
Applies from: 1 January 2026


Amount

From 1 January 2026 an employer who hires a first employee gets a reduction of employer social security contributions of €2,000 per quarter. The reduction applies for at most 20 quarters.

Previous rule

Hires before 1 January 2026 keep the old amount of €3,100 per quarter.
