# Starter employers – payroll guide (2022)

Owner: Marc Dubois · Legal Payroll BE · February 2022

## First employee

A company that hires its very first employee pays far less social security. The reduction is €3,100 per quarter and has no end date. Apply it in the DmfA under reduction code 3410.

## Second to sixth employee

Smaller reductions apply. See the NSSO tables.
