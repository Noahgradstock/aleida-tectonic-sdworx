GIFTS TO EMPLOYEES ON SPECIAL OCCASIONS

Reference: CIR/GIFT/2026
Applies from: 1 January 2026


End-of-year gifts

A gift in kind, in cash or as a gift voucher for Christmas, New Year or Saint Nicholas is free of tax and social security up to €50 per employee per year, plus €40 per dependent child.

Previous amount

Until 31 December 2025 the limit was €40 per employee.
