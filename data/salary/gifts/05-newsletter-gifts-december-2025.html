End-of-year gifts: a higher limit from 2026

Dear client,

From 1 January 2026 the tax-free limit for gifts to your staff rises to €50 per employee per year. The amount per dependent child stays at €40.

For gifts given in December 2025 the old limit still applies. This newsletter is fictional and was written for a demo.
