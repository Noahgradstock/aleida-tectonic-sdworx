Auxiliary Joint Committee for white-collar workers (PC 200)
Collective agreement on shift and night work (fictional, for the demo)

NOTICE - NIGHT WORK PREMIUM FROM 1 JANUARY 2024

Publication date: 12 December 2023
Reference: PC200/NIGHT/2024-01


1. Amount

From 1 January 2024 the night work premium is €1.98 per hour worked between
20:00 and 06:00.


2. Duration

This agreement is concluded for an indefinite period. The amount is reviewed
by the joint committee every two years.
