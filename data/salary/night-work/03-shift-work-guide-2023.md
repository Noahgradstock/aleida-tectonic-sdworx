# Shift and night work – payroll guide (2023)

Owner: Koen Maes · Payroll BE · March 2023

## Which hours count as night work

Hours worked between 20:00 and 06:00 count as night work for white-collar employees in PC 200.

## Premium

Pay a night work premium of €1.98 per hour on top of the normal hourly wage. Use wage code 1420 in the payroll engine.

## Rest

Leave at least 11 hours between two shifts. Night shifts may not exceed 9 hours.
