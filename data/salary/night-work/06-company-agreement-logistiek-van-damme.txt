COMPANY AGREEMENT ON SHIFT WORK – LOGISTIEK VAN DAMME NV (fictional, for the demo)

Between Logistiek Van Damme NV (employer) and the trade union delegation.
Signed in Ghent on 18 November 2025. Uploaded by An Peeters.


Article 1. Night hours

Night hours are the hours worked between 22:00 and 06:00.

Article 2. Night premium

For every night hour the employee receives a night work premium of €2.60 per hour. This premium is not indexed.

Article 3. Duration

This agreement applies from 1 January 2026 for an indefinite period.
