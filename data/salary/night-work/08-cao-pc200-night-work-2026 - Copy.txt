Collective agreement on shift and night work (fictional, for the demo)

NOTICE - NIGHT WORK PREMIUM FROM 1 JANUARY 2026

Publication date: 10 December 2025
Reference: PC200/NIGHT/2026-01


1. Scope

This agreement applies to employers and white-collar employees under PC 200
who perform work between 20:00 and 06:00 as part of a shift or night regime.


2. Amount

From 1 January 2026 the night work premium is €2.15 per hour worked between
20:00 and 06:00. The amount replaces the premium of €1.98 per hour that applied
from 1 January 2024.


3. Company agreements

A company agreement may grant a higher premium. It may not grant a lower one.
