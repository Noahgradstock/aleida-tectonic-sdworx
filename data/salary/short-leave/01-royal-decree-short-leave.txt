SHORT LEAVE (KLEIN VERLET / PETIT CHÔMAGE) – FAMILY EVENTS

Reference: KB/KV/2024-01
Applies from: 1 January 2024


Own wedding

For the employee's own wedding the employee keeps the normal wage for 2 days, to be chosen in the week of the wedding or the week after.

Other events

Wedding of a child, brother or sister: the day of the wedding. Death of a partner, child or parent: 3 days.

Before 1 January 2024 the employee had 3 days for an own wedding.
