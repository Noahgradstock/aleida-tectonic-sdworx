SUNDAY WORK – PAY AND COMPENSATORY REST

Reference: WAS/SUN/2022-07
Applies from: 1 July 2022


Pay

Hours worked on a Sunday, where Sunday work is allowed, are paid with a premium of 100% on top of the normal wage. Before 1 July 2022 the premium was 75%.

Compensatory rest

The employee gets a compensatory rest day within the six days after the Sunday.

Sector agreements

A sector or company agreement may set a higher premium.
