CHAPTER 9  WORK ON SUNDAYS AND PUBLIC HOLIDAYS


9.1  Sunday premium

Hours worked on a Sunday are paid with a premium of 75% of the normal hourly wage. Enter the hours under wage code 1510.

9.2  Compensatory rest

The employee is entitled to a compensatory rest day within six days.
