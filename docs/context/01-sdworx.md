# SD Worx: the company and its AI direction

## The company
- Belgian (Antwerp), HR, payroll and workforce management. 80+ years.
- 10 000+ employees, 100 000+ customers, operations in 27 countries, payroll for ~6 M employees, payroll reach in 100+ countries.
- Core users for our solutions: **payroll consultants** who manage a portfolio of client companies, and the clients' HR admins.

## AI strategy (relevant for "Fit")
- **Multi-agent AI for payroll consultants** (built with Yuma). Agents with specific roles: one gathers information, one helps with payroll processing, customer service, knowledge lookup, administration.
  - Pilot → rollout in Belgian SME payroll teams, ~500 consultants by late summer 2026. Then more segments, the Netherlands, Finland.
  - Message: automate the repetitive work, the consultant focuses on advice and client contact. **Humans direct the agents and have the final say** (control, quality, decisions). Faster, more consistent service, compliance.
- **Internal pan-European hackathon 2025:** 113 developers, 11 countries, 23 teams.
  - **Winner:** AI payroll agent that turns **legal documents → system-ready validation rules**.
  - **Runner-up:** AI HR admin agent that turns **unstructured customer emails → direct system actions**.
  - Both moved on to MVP.

## What it says about what they like
- Concrete, well-scoped agents in the payroll consultant's daily work. Not general chatbots.
- Unstructured in → structured, verifiable out (rules, actions).
- Human-in-the-loop, compliance, explainability.
- Scalable across countries (different legislation per country = a natural "router" dimension).
