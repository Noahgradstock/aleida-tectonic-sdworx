# KBC track (for information, we are doing SD Worx)

KBC: one of Belgium's largest banks (banking, investment, insurance).

**Round 1 case:** Imagine KBC understands exactly what the customer needs and responds at exactly the right moment. Think first without limits, then about how it scales to **2 300 000+ customers**.
- Which signals show what customers need?
- How are customers recognised from situation, behaviour and intent?
- How is the experience adapted automatically per customer, across products, services and channels?
- "Not a new feature. A vision + PoC for scalable personalisation."

Relevance for us: the same router/signal pattern (signals → segment/intent → action) applies. KBC has its own AI assistant ("Kate") in the app.
