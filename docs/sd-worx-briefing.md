# SD Worx: background briefing

Compiled by the team from public sources before the final (18 October 2026). Use it for context and for speaking SD Worx's language. Check anything marked "unverified" before saying it on stage.

## In one minute

- **Who:** Belgian HR, Pay & Time company, HQ Antwerp, founded 1945 as "Sociale Dienst", renamed SD Worx in 1985. CEO Kobe Verdonck (since 2019), chairman Filip Dierckx.
- **Scale:** €1.307 bn revenue in 2025 (+10.7%), €679.1 m in H1 2026 (+4.6%, like-for-like +3.5%). Around 100,000 clients, about 6 million people paid per month, about 10,000 colleagues, 27 countries, about 1.7 million mysdworx users.
- **Brand (June 2026):** "Built for how Europe works". SD Worx "makes work work". Tone: dependable and practical.
- **Their language:** "local expertise at European scale", "turn complexity into confidence", "powered by technology and backed by people".
- **Values ("be a spark"):** commitment drives us forward · one for all, all for one · Dear Customer · we believe in each other · the road is open.

In three sentences: SD Worx helps organisations across Europe with HR, payroll and working time, the administration that keeps people employed, paid and supported. It serves around 100,000 organisations in 27 countries and pays around 6 million people. Its strength is turning complicated local employment rules into reliable everyday processes by combining technology with local experts.

It is **not** day-to-day people management (coaching, feedback). It **is** the admin, compliance and expertise behind having employees.

## Business

- **Business lines:** People Solutions (HR & payroll, €567.5 m in H1 2026) and Staffing & Career Solutions (€112.0 m).
- **Payroll / Pay:** calculation, local statutory reporting, managed payroll and full outsourcing, rewards and flexible benefits, tax and legal advice.
- **HR / HCM:** core HR, onboarding, absence, talent, people analytics. SD Worx Buddy for SMEs.
- **Time & workforce management:** clocking, leave, shifts and scheduling, mainly through Protime (which acquired Checks in 2026).
- **mysdworx:** web and mobile entry point for employees, managers and HR: payslips, leave, expenses, overtime, alerts. The expense assistant fills in fields from a photo of a receipt.
- **Large enterprises + SAP:** Innovapay adds local payroll to SAP landscapes (about 850 SAP-certified experts).
- **Three service models:** SaaS (client processes) · managed payroll (shared responsibility) · full outsourcing / BPO (SD Worx runs it under SLAs).
- **Recognition:** named a "Leader" by NelsonHall (payroll services) and Everest Group (EMEA multi-country payroll) in 2025.

## Strategy

- Europe-first growth: organic growth plus selective acquisitions, then standardising platforms.
- Move from periodic payroll to **continuous, AI-assisted HR + Pay + Time**. Real-time payroll is live in Germany; Belgium is an early market for the new cloud-native platform.
- **Growth by acquisition** means every acquired company brings its own systems, documents and ways of working, which SD Worx then has to integrate and standardise.

## AI already in house

- **Legal Watch** (April 2026, built with Faktion): AI monitors hundreds of legal sources, and SD Worx experts validate before publishing through mysdworx. Rolling out first in DE, LU, ES, SE and NL. Pattern: AI drafts, human experts validate.
- **Agentic AI in payroll (June 2026):** consultants each manage seven or more AI agents (customer interactions, knowledge retrieval, payroll execution), with humans validating. Rollout starts with 500+ consultants in Belgian SME payroll teams.
- **Multi-agent AI customer service:** being scaled. A 2024 planning agent reportedly engaged with 80% of tickets and resolved 40% autonomously (unverified company case study figure).
- **Other:** Data & Insights AI agent, Protime reporting assistant, AI-assisted coding.
- **mysdworx roadmap:** MFA, Quick Actions, onboarding widget, conversational document Q&A. The SD Worx Assistant in mysdworx was built by In The Pocket, which co-organises Tectonic.
- **Internal 2025 hackathon (unverified):** the winner turned legal documents into payroll validation rules; the runner-up turned customer emails into HR actions.

## Challenges

**SD Worx:**
- Payroll errors and slow corrections reported by some clients.
- Support and escalation: confusion over whether to go to the employer's HR or to SD Worx.
- Platform and UX: login issues, missing profile data, modules that feel complex or dated.
- Legacy and integration debt from many acquired systems.
- **Knowledge fragmentation:** many teams, countries, tools and acquisitions mean scattered, duplicated and outdated knowledge.
- Slower like-for-like growth in 2026 (3.5%), which adds pressure on cost-to-serve and efficiency through AI.
- Security: a cyberattack in April 2023 forced SD Worx to shut down its UK & Ireland systems.

**Industry-wide:**
- Regulatory fragmentation: tax, social security, sector agreements and EU rules move at different speeds.
- Fragmented data across HR, time, payroll and finance; late changes after the cut-off.
- Manual errors under deadline pressure: about 1 in 3 employees had a payroll error in the past two years (industry survey).
- **Commoditised chat:** every vendor has an AI assistant, so value moves to grounded data, permissions, trust and safely completed actions.
- Talent shortage: fewer payroll experts and high volumes make reusable knowledge more valuable.
- Highly sensitive personal data; employees expect instant, mobile, transparent answers.
